This is an **exact finite enumeration within the stated abstract rank-two home-return model**, not a full recursive supplier invoice. Source of the signed 27/1 lemma: `signed_edge_census.py`, with the exceptional demand at the complement of the *antipodal* cube edge. Enumeration source: `single_sign_optimum.py`.

The full 12-edge bank uniquely achieves the minimum ADD count 288. No universal single choice follows until physical per-helper ownership, frame transport, and scalar costs are known.

The 8-helper case is validated by a *literal symbolic 104-target trace for one genuine signed E8 decoder*, including source home returns and zero cleanup. The 7168-decoder signed incidence lemma is checked separately by exhaustive integer arithmetic; **neither certifies native Clifford/phase eligibility or an all-size theorem**.
