## Most promising next physical acceptance gate

Upstream open #5243 calculates that **one actually legal native F1+F1→F2 paid fusion per each of 18 full p16 calls** would alter the whole paid profile by `-36 H1+18 H2` with unchanged targets. At κ=0.0030220, the old whole moment is **−0.05540972462695** and the hypothetical changed whole moment **+0.01992021942696**. The finite-compiler/outer applicability is a *separate source assumption*. No actual two-register physical merger exists yet in this research.

The exact selected full word contains 671 immediately adjacent pairs of rank-one climbs in each orientation. They are always on **different persistent L registers**, never on one register. Constructing the required joint two-register recursive call needs a **fully costed** encoder/owner/tensor transport, two independent arbitrary-input behaviors, phase-correct dual and cleanup, and correct return interface. Do not silently count two calls as one on the basis of their adjacent time position or tensor algebra.

First high-value experiment: take one adjacent window, write the exact two-register source/target map with arbitrary independent spectators; enumerate small native Clifford block candidates and reject or admit a legal one-child rank-two encoding **including source packing and outputs**. If no one-child witness exists, try a changed scalar DAG and a new controller that allow one-register coalescence, charging both transformations. If any success, patch all 18 native occurrences, rerun every 632-column direction, all relative phases, complete p16 job invoice, independent rational moments, ordinary BIT and all 54 strict plus four functional outer checks, and the uniform all-size compiler/precision/ownership/tape implications.
